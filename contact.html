Frequently Asked Questions


How do I find events?
You can browse the event calendar on our homepage or use the search bar to filter by date and category.

How do reminders work?
When viewing a specific event, click the 'Set Reminder' button to receive an on-screen notification before the event starts.

How do I locate a venue?
Navigate to our interactive campus map and search for the venue name to see its precise location.
